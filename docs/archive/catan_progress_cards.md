# Catan: Cities & Knights – Complete Progress Card Reference

## 1. SCIENCE (GREEN) PROGRESS CARDS

### Alchemist
**Text:** Choose the results of the production dice and then roll them normally.  
**Explanation:** Before rolling, you declare exactly what the two production dice will show. The event die still rolls normally.

### Medicine
**Text:** Pay 2 ore and 1 grain instead of 3 ore and 2 grain to upgrade a settlement to a city.  
**Explanation:** Reduces cost once for a single upgrade.

### Mining
**Text:** Take 2 ore for each mountain hex you own adjacent to a city.  
**Explanation:** Cities only, robber blocks if present.

### Irrigation
**Text:** Take 2 grain for each field hex you own adjacent to a city.  
**Explanation:** Cities only.

### Crane
**Text:** Reduce the cost of your next city improvement by 1 commodity of the improvement’s type.  
**Explanation:** Applies to one improvement, cannot reduce below zero.

### Engineer
**Text:** Build a city wall for free.  
**Explanation:** Place one wall immediately.

### Inventor
**Text:** Swap any two number tokens not on an opponent’s hex.  
**Explanation:** Cards cannot move tokens connected to other players’ settlements or cities.

### Printer
**Text:** Gain 1 victory point.  
**Explanation:** Permanent VP.

## 2. TRADE (YELLOW) PROGRESS CARDS

### Commercial Harbor
**Text:** Exchange 1 commodity for 2 different commodities from one opponent.  
**Explanation:** Opponent chooses which 2 to give; they must comply if they have them.

### Merchant
**Text:** Place the Merchant on any hex to gain a trade advantage and 1 VP.  
**Explanation:** Grants 1 VP and 2:1 trade for that resource; you also gain 1 gold when that hex produces.

### Merchant Fleet
**Text:** For one turn, choose a resource to trade at a 2:1 rate.  
**Explanation:** Applies only this turn.

### Merchant’s Favor
**Text:** Take one commodity from each player with more trade improvements than you.  
**Explanation:** Each qualifying player gives you one commodity of their choice.

### Resource Monopoly
**Text:** Name one resource; each player gives you 1 if they have any.  
**Explanation:** Each player gives exactly one card.

### Commercial Monopoly
**Text:** Name one commodity; each player gives you 1 if they have any.  
**Explanation:** Same as the above but for commodities.

### Master Merchant
**Text:** Choose a player with more victory points than you; take 2 cards of your choice from their hand.  
**Explanation:** You pick the exact cards.

### Trade Advantage
**Text:** Take one resource of your choice from each opponent who produced this turn.  
**Explanation:** Applies to players who gained production from the most recent roll.

## 3. POLITICS (BLUE) PROGRESS CARDS

### Bishop
**Text:** Move the robber as if you rolled a 7; each opponent on the new hex gives you 1 resource.  
**Explanation:** They choose what to give.

### Constable
**Text:** Gain 1 victory point.  
**Explanation:** Permanent VP.

### Deserter
**Text:** Choose an opponent; remove one of their knights and place a knight of the same strength for yourself.  
**Explanation:** Your knight enters inactive.

### Diplomat
**Text:** Remove any open road; you may place it as your own.  
**Explanation:** Only roads with an open end may be targeted.

### Intrigue
**Text:** Move an opponent’s knight that is adjacent to one of your knights.  
**Explanation:** You reposition the knight to any legal adjacent intersection.

### Road Building
**Text:** Build two roads for free.  
**Explanation:** Standard base-game effect.

### Saboteur
**Text:** All opponents with more victory points than you must discard half their cards, rounded down.  
**Explanation:** Includes resources and commodities.

### Smith
**Text:** Promote two of your knights for free.  
**Explanation:** Promotions must follow your tech level.

### Warlord
**Text:** Activate all your knights for free.  
**Explanation:** Very strong before barbarians attack.

### Wedding
**Text:** Each opponent with more victory points than you must give you 1 resource or commodity.  
**Explanation:** One card per qualifying opponent.

## 4. PROGRESS CARD RULES SUMMARY

### Drawing Cards
You draw a progress card when:
- The event die shows a color matching one of your city improvement tracks.
- You have at least one improvement in that color.
- At level 3+, you draw two and keep one.

### Hand Limit
You may hold **4 progress cards** max at the end of your turn.

### Play Timing
Most cards are played on your turn before rolling the dice unless the card provides a reaction effect.
