# 🌿 SCIENCE (GREEN) PROGRESS CARDS  
*(Pages 13–14 of rulebook)*

## **Alchemy (2x)**
Play at start of your turn, before rolling. Choose results of the production dice; roll the event die normally.

## **Crane (2x)**
Build one city improvement for 1 commodity less.

## **Engineering (1x)**
Build one city wall for free.

## **Inventor (2x)**
Swap any two number tokens except 2, 6, 8, or 12.

## **Irrigation (2x)**
Take 2 wheat for each fields hex adjacent to one of your buildings.

## **Medicine (2x)**
Upgrade a settlement to a city for 2 ore + 1 wheat.

## **Mining (2x)**
Take 2 ore for each mountains hex adjacent to one of your buildings.

## **Road Building (2x)**
Build two roads for free.

## **Smithing (2x)**
Promote up to two knights for free.

## **Printing (1x) — VP**
Play immediately. Worth 1 VP.

# 💛 TRADE (YELLOW) PROGRESS CARDS  
*(Pages 14–15)*

## **Commercial Harbor (2x)**
Offer 1 resource to each player; each must give you 1 commodity if they have one. Otherwise, you take your resource back.

## **Guild Dues (2x)**
Choose a player with more VPs than you. Look at their hand and take any 2 cards.

## **Merchant (6x)**
Place Merchant on a resource hex adjacent to one of your buildings. While you control it: 2:1 trade of that resource +1 VP.

## **Merchant Fleet (2x)**
Choose 1 resource or commodity. Trade it at 2:1 with supply for the rest of this turn.

## **Resource Monopoly (4x)**
Name a resource. Each player must give you up to 2 of that resource.

## **Trade Monopoly (2x)**
Name a commodity. Each player must give you 1 of that commodity if they have it.

---

# 💙 POLITICS (BLUE) PROGRESS CARDS  
*(Pages 15–16)*

## **Diplomat (2x)**
Remove an open road. If it is yours, you may rebuild one road for free.

## **Espionage (3x)**
Look at another player’s progress cards; take 1.

## **Encouragement (2x)**
Activate all your knights for free.

## **Intrigue (2x)**
Displace a knight on an intersection connected to one of your routes.

## **Taxation (2x)**
Move the robber. Steal 1 random card from each player with a building on that hex.

## **Treason (2x)**
Choose a player; they remove a knight. You place a knight of equal or lower strength with same status.

## **Wedding (2x)**
Each player with more VPs than you must give you 2 cards of their choice.

## **Sabotage (2x)**
Players with equal or more VPs discard half their resource/commodity cards.

## **Constitution (1x) — VP**
Play immediately. Worth 1 VP.

---

# Global Progress Card Rules
- Max 4 progress cards in hand at end of turn.
- VP cards are not kept in hand.
- Only **Alchemy** is played before rolling.
- All others played after rolling on your turn.
- Multiple cards may be played per turn.
